## Fan Control (Fans tab)

The fullscreen / touch+gamepad curve editor (graph, Fan Stop, point list,
create/reset presets) is ported from
[armada-os/armada#260](https://github.com/armada-os/armada/pull/260)
by **Xtreme976**.

It was adapted to Batocera's `qcom-fan` so Silent / Auto / Aggressive stay
shared with Control Center (Home+A) instead of driving a second daemon.
